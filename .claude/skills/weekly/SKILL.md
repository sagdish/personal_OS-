If `modules.weekly` is off in `os.yaml`, say so and stop.

Read, in this order, from the journal folder named in `os.yaml`: the last seven files in `reads/`,
the tail of `wins.md` (if on), the last seven `daily/` notes, `goals.md` (if on), `challenge.md`
(if on), the last seven `inbox/` day files. Nothing from git.

Write `<journal>/weeks/<YYYY-Www>.md` (the ISO week): what they did, in their words where they
wrote any; what moved in each area; what stayed still, said once, without a count; one question if
their words raise one. No score, no streak, no grade, no plan for next week unless they asked for
one. If this week's file already exists, ask before rewriting it.

Say one line: where it is.
