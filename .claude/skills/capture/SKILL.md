Take what they just said, exactly as they said it, and run:

    node bin/os.mjs capture "their words"

Keep their line breaks. If the text holds double quotes, wrap it in single quotes instead.

Do not organise, route, rewrite or improve it. Do not ask a question. Reply with one line: what was
captured. Routing happens later, by the daily read or by /triage, and nothing is ever deleted.
